The Story

Between shadow
and light

The Autumn/Winter collection finds its muse in the liminal hour — that breath between dusk and dark when the world suspends itself in amber and emerald. Each piece is a meditation on restraint. On the beauty of the unuttered.

Crafted across four ateliers in Paris, Milan, and Kyoto, every silhouette is born from a singular obsession: that luxury is felt, never announced.
